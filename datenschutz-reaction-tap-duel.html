Die Daten dienen der Anzeigenauslieferung, Messung und technischen Absicherung. Reaction Tap Duel privacy information

Version 2.2 for iOS and iPadOS is a local game with no app account. It uses Google AdMob for interstitials and Google's User Messaging Platform for privacy choices. The app checks the privacy status before requesting ads and displays a privacy options entry point when required.

Depending on settings and choices, Google may process network information, approximate location derived from IP address, device identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostics for ads, measurement and security. See Google's partner services privacy information. For privacy requests, contact Michael Orend at the address above.
